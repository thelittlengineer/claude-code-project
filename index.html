QUESTIONS

The things people ask me first.

How long does it really take?

Two weeks from our call to your site being live, as long as I have your text and pictures. If you do not have them, I write the words and we sort the pictures out together, and it is still two weeks.

What if I do not like it?

You see the design before it goes live and we change it until it is right. Those changes are part of the 300. I do not bill you by the round.

Do I own it?

Yes. The site, the domain, and the hosting account are all in your name. If you ever want to work with someone else, you take everything with you.

What happens after it goes live?

I fix anything that breaks, free, for the first month. After that you can call me for changes, or make the small ones yourself. I show you how before I hand it over.

I have no photos and no text. Is that a problem?

No, and most people do not. I write the words for you and we sort the pictures out together on the call.

Why is it only 300 dollars?

Because I am new and I want real work to show. The price goes to 500 once my book is full. Nothing about the website is smaller because of it.
